Assert X-Frame-Options is ABSENT, which is what Q37 decided

`phase6-compliance.spec.ts` asserted `x-frame-options: DENY` on both private
surfaces. Q37 - decided by the owner on 2026-08-28 - removed that header from
the product on purpose. `CONSTANT_SECURITY_HEADERS` carries the full reasoning:
framing is governed by `frame-ancestors` alone, CSP3 defines it as superseding
the header when both are present, and it is the only one of the two that can
express the single exception Track 11.D needs - the live preview iframe.
Next's `headers()` APPENDS rather than replaces, so a per-path `SAMEORIGIN`
would have shipped both values and the browser would take the stricter one.

Q37 updated `tests/unit/phase6-security-headers.test.ts`. This e2e case was the
copy nobody updated, and it had been failing on both surfaces ever since -
unread, because the suite could not run anywhere.

Asserted ABSENT rather than deleted. A header re-added by a well-meaning future
change would ship two framing rules whose interaction is exactly what Q37
rejected, and nothing else would catch it; `frame-ancestors 'none'` is already
asserted four lines above, so the coverage this replaces is not lost.

Fourth of the ten first-run e2e failures. The previous three are confirmed green
on CI: both font-preload cases and the Node-side DNS lookup.
